# Payung Terms of Service

**Version 1.0 · Effective 22 September 2026**

These terms explain what Payung does for you, what you agree to do, and how cancellations and problems are handled.

> Payung is a **matching service** between care recipients and caregivers. It is not a healthcare provider. In a medical emergency, call **1669** immediately.

---

## 1. What Payung does

- Helps you find and match with a caregiver suited to your needs and area
- Collects payment on the caregiver's behalf, and refunds it as described in section 4
- Verifies each caregiver's identity before they can accept jobs
- Handles complaints when something goes wrong

Care is provided **directly between you and the caregiver**. Payung does not provide medical treatment or diagnosis.

---

## 2. Your account

1. You must be **20 years of age or older** to sign up
2. The information you enter must be accurate and up to date, especially health information, which the caregiver relies on to care for you safely
3. If you enter information for someone else, you must be their legal guardian or authorised representative, or have their consent
4. Keep your password secret. Anything done through your account is treated as done by you

---

## 3. Bookings and payment

- The price is **always shown before you pay**
- Payment is by card or PromptPay, processed by Omise
- A booking is confirmed only once a caregiver accepts it and payment succeeds
- If no caregiver accepts, or payment is not made in time, the system closes the booking automatically and **refunds in full** anything already paid

---

## 4. Cancellations and refunds

| Situation | Refund |
| --- | --- |
| You cancel **at least 24 hours** before the start time | Full refund |
| You cancel less than 24 hours before the start time | Currently a full refund. Any change will be announced in advance under section 9 |
| The caregiver does not show up | Full refund |
| The system closes the booking because it ran out of time | Full refund |

Refunds go back to the original payment method. How long they take to arrive depends on your bank or card issuer. Payung covers payment processing fees.

---

## 5. During the service

- The caregiver checks in and out through the app, recording their location to confirm they actually arrived
- The caregiver may record care notes and take photos during the job so you and your family can follow along
- Please make the place safe to work in, and share everything the caregiver needs to know to provide care

---

## 6. What is not allowed

- Giving false information or using someone else's identity
- Harassing, harming, or discriminating against a caregiver
- Using the service for anything unlawful
- Trying to access the system or other people's data without permission

---

## 7. Complaints and disputes

Report a problem from the booking details page in the app. Our team reviews it and lets you know the outcome within **3 days** of receiving it. If the outcome involves money, we refund according to the decision.

---

## 8. Reviews

Reviews must reflect real experiences and be respectful. We may hide reviews that contain personal data, abusive language, or false statements.

---

## 9. Suspension and changes to these terms

- If you breach section 6, we may suspend or close your account. Bookings that have not started are cancelled and refunded under section 4
- If we change anything substantive in these terms, we will publish a new version and ask you to accept it again before you continue using the service
