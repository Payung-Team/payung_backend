# Privacy Notice for Care Recipients

**Version 1.0 · Effective 22 September 2026**

This notice explains what information Payung collects about you, what we do with it, who sees it, how long we keep it, and what rights you have.

> This notice applies to **care recipients and their families**. Caregivers have a separate notice.

---

## 1. Who holds your information

The Payung team is the data controller. For anything about your personal data, contact **privacy@payung.app**.

---

## 2. What we collect

### 2.1 General information

| Information | When we collect it |
| --- | --- |
| First name, last name, nickname | When you first fill in your details |
| Email, phone number | At sign-up |
| Address, sub-district, district, province, postal code | At onboarding and when booking |
| Profile photo | Only if you choose to upload one |
| Booking and payment history | Every time you book |

### 2.2 Health information — data the law protects with extra care

Health information is **sensitive personal data under Section 26 of Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562**. We collect it only after you give **separate, explicit consent**.

- Age, gender, weight, height
- Mobility level
- Medical conditions, regular medications, drug and food allergies, blood type
- Care notes and preferred hospital
- Care logs and photos recorded by the caregiver during a job

### 2.3 Information collected automatically

- IP address and browser type, **only at the moment you give consent**, as evidence that the consent genuinely happened
- Last sign-in time
- Location at caregiver check-in and check-out, to confirm they actually arrived

**We do not store** full card numbers or full bank account numbers. Payments are processed by Omise; our system keeps only the last 4 digits so you can tell which card you used.

---

## 3. What we use it for

| Purpose | Legal basis |
| --- | --- |
| Creating and maintaining your account | Performance of a contract |
| Matching a caregiver suited to the condition and area | **Explicit consent** (health data) |
| Sending your details to the caregiver who accepts the job | **Explicit consent** |
| Taking payment, issuing receipts, and refunding cancellations | Contract and legal obligation |
| Notifying you of booking status by email and in-app | Performance of a contract |
| Investigating complaints and resolving disputes | Legitimate interest |
| Sending news and promotions | **Consent** (optional — declining changes nothing else) |

We do **not sell** your data, and we do **not use** health information for marketing.

---

## 4. Who sees your information

| Recipient | What they see | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The caregiver who accepts your job | Your name, the service address, and the health information you provided | Only after accepting |
| Caregivers who have not accepted | Nothing about you | — |
| Members of your family group | Your bookings, job status and care recipient details | Only if you consent to family group sharing |
| Omise (payment processor) | What is needed to charge a card or PromptPay | At payment |
| Supabase (database and file storage provider) | Stores data on our instructions | Throughout the service |
| Government authorities | Only what the law requires | On a lawful order |

---

## 5. How long we keep it

| Information | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| General account information | As long as the account is open |
| Health information and care logs | **2 years** after the account closes, then deleted |
| Financial records and receipts | **5 years**, as accounting and tax law requires |
| Consent records | **5 years**, to prove the consent genuinely happened |

Consent records deliberately outlive the data itself — deleting them together would leave us unable to prove that consent was properly obtained.

---

## 6. Your rights

You have the following rights. To use any of them, contact **privacy@payung.app**.

1. **Access** the information we hold about you, and request a copy
2. **Correct** information that is wrong or out of date
3. **Erase** your information, or have it made non-identifiable
4. **Restrict** how we use it, temporarily
5. **Object** to collection or use in certain cases
6. **Port** your data to another provider in a machine-readable format
7. **Withdraw consent** at any time
8. **Complain** to the Personal Data Protection Committee

We respond within **30 days** of receiving a request.

**Withdrawing consent** does not affect processing that already happened. If you withdraw consent for health information, you will no longer be able to book a caregiver, because we would not hold enough information to match one safely.

---

## 7. How we protect it

- Data is encrypted in transit, and the database restricts access row by row
- Photos and documents are kept in private storage, opened only through short-lived links that expire — never public URLs
- Uploaded photos have their location and camera metadata stripped before storage
- Staff access is limited to what their role requires

---

## 8. Children and persons under guardianship

This service is for people aged 20 and over. If the care recipient is a minor or a person under guardianship, whoever fills in the information must be the legal guardian and confirms they are entitled to consent on that person's behalf.

---

## 9. When this notice changes

If we change anything substantive — adding a purpose, adding a recipient, or changing how long we keep data — we will publish a new version and ask for your consent again before you continue using the service.

Fixing typos or reformatting the page does not create a new version and does not trigger a new consent request.
